# The Protocol domain rename plan

The live canonical domain remains `peptideprotocolau.io` until a replacement is purchased, configured, and tested.

## Recommended domains

Check current availability and trade-mark suitability before purchase, in this order:

1. `theprotocol.com.au` — clearest Australian commercial identity.
2. `theprotocol.au` — shortest Australian option.
3. `theprotocolresearch.com.au` — descriptive fallback with lower brand ambiguity.
4. `protocolresearch.au` — concise fallback if the preferred names are unavailable.

## Migration checklist

1. Purchase the chosen domain and configure it on the hosting platform without removing the current domain.
2. Add the new support mailbox and aliases; preserve `support@peptideprotocolau.io` during transition.
3. Change the canonical site URL, metadata base, sitemap, robots, social links, checkout return URLs, QR targets, and transactional email sender together.
4. Add permanent one-to-one `301` redirects from every old URL to the same path on the new domain; do not redirect all traffic to the home page.
5. Update MoonPay/Stripe or other payment allowlists, Convex/webhook URLs, analytics, Search Console, merchant profiles, legal documents, invoices, receipts, packing slips, COAs, and print artwork.
6. Verify TLS, checkout, email delivery, webhooks, deep links, sitemap, structured data, and canonical tags before announcing the change.
7. Submit the new sitemap and Search Console change-of-address request, then monitor crawl errors, rankings, analytics, payment failures, and email bounces.
8. Keep the old domain, redirects, and email aliases active for at least 12 months; longer is preferable.
